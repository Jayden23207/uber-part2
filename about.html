About Us

Our story

Uber started in San Francisco in 2009. It began as an app that let people book a ride with their phone. Since then, Uber has grown to offer food delivery, package delivery, and business travel too.

Our mission

We want to make it easier for the world to move.

Who uses Uber

  • People who need a ride around town
  • People who order food or groceries
  • Drivers and couriers who want flexible work
  • Companies that manage travel for their staff
